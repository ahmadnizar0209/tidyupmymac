Privacy Policy

LapangDiska · Last updated 21 October 2026

LapangDiska does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your Mac

  • Scans run on your Mac. File names, paths, sizes, scan results, your exclusions and the record of what was moved to the Trash are stored only on your Mac, inside the app's own container.
  • The home folder access you grant is used only to measure and tidy that folder on your Mac. You can revoke it by removing the app.

What the app does not do

  • There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app makes no network connections of its own.

Purchases

The Full Version is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the purchase; the developer receives no personal or payment information.
