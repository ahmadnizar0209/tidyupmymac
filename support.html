TidyUpMyMac for macOS

Nothing was found

On first launch, choose your home folder when TidyUpMyMac asks. To choose it again, remove and reinstall the app.

Put things back

Everything TidyUpMyMac removes goes to the Trash. Open the Trash in Finder, select the items and choose File › Put Back. With Full Disk Access granted (System Settings › Privacy & Security), Undo also works inside the app.

Restore a purchase

Choose TidyUpMyMac › Full Version… › Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
